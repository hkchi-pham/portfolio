About me ↘

Who I am and
why it matters

The one thing I want you to remember about me is how I’m drawn to questions that make me want to keep digging.

My interest in Computer Science has grown from the wow factor of seeing technology for the first time, to exploring how algorithms shape what we see, to studying the mechanisms inside modern AI systems.

Throughout this journey, I enjoy learning independently, reading beyond the curriculum, and turning questions into experiments and projects. Whether I’m investigating transformer models or building an application, I’m most interested in diving into why they work, but more especially what impact it could make.
