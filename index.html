I want you to remember that I’m drawn to questions that make me keep digging.

My interest in Computer Science has grown from ‘the wow factor’ of seeing technology for the first time, to exploring how algorithms shape what we see and finally, to studying the mechanisms inside modern AI systems.

Throughout this journey, I have enjoyed learning independently, reading beyond the curriculum, and turning questions into experiments and projects. [ Leadership & service ]

House Captain, Hanoi House

BVIS Hanoi · after deputy roles in Years 9 and 10

I grew into house leadership step by step: deputy house captain for Key Stage 3 in Year 9, house deputy in Year 10, and house captain in Year 12. Along the way I helped coordinate Hanoi House’s introductory performance at school assembly, and supported students in organising our Christmas market and team-building activities.

As captain, I built on what Hanoi House does best. We are not the house that wins at sport or music, but our members are enthusiastic learners and committed teammates, so I focused our year on house points, with a house quiz every Friday and regular house competitions. I led Hanoi House to House Point Champion in Primary and second place in Secondary.

2025 — 26

EDIB Prefect

BVIS Hanoi · Equality, Diversity, Inclusion and Belonging

Our school wants to put more focus on equality, diversity, inclusion and belonging across the community. As EDIB prefect, I am helping design a PSHE lesson on the topic, built around engaging activities where every student takes a turn to discuss, role-play and contribute.

After finishing Soul Garden, I am planning an ECA for Term 2a. Building the app has shown me how much my own habits of emotional regulation, journalling and talking things through, had improved my life, and I wanted to help other students recognise their emotions and manage their moods. To prepare it, I am learning grounding techniques and strategies for identifying emotions, in consultation with the school counsellor.

I am also working with an Academic Prefect on a website that pairs younger students with approved older mentors by subject, with a place to share notes and study resources and subject group chats for discussion.

2026 — now

Co-founder, STEM Club

BVIS Hanoi · 8 members

Our club teaches younger students what it is like to be a scientist. We split into teams, recruit younger students into each one through our ECA, and every team writes a research paper on a topic it chooses. Working alongside us, the younger students learn the full research process: forming a hypothesis, setting up an experiment, weighing limitations and alternatives, interpreting results and writing the report.

Each team, founders and younger members together, is currently applying for the CREST Silver Award (see our echo-chamber project, where I was research mentor), but the award is not our main goal. What matters more is giving younger students hands-on experience of innovation and creativity.
